Interpretation and next tests

Analyst findings apply to the explicitly cited scope, independently of the filters above. Hypotheses remain provisional until their tests support them.

Reading the measures

Volume and attributionOffers count queue workload, including repeat offers and transfers. Flow counts represent executions. Neither establishes unique customers; separate aggregates do not form a customer funnel.

Service levelUses Genesys oServiceLevel and the organization's configured denominator. Combined SL needs numerator and denominator. Averaging percentages is incorrect.

ASA and AHTASA uses time-to-answer sum / answered count. AHT uses handle-time sum / handled count. Durations are converted from milliseconds to seconds. These populations differ.

Daily changesOnly adjacent complete local days receive deltas. Rate changes are percentage points; other metrics show absolute changes. Partial days and uncertain source coverage are excluded.

Channel differencesMedia stay separate. Callback queue aggregates do not measure callback reach or bridge success. Voice abandonment assumptions must not be applied to web messaging.

Timing and completenessOffers, answers and completed handles can appear in different intervals. Missing buckets remain gaps; omitted metrics remain N/A. No staffing or root cause is inferred from aggregates alone.

Evidence and coverage

Local analytical artifact. No external scripts, fonts or tracking. Raw source hashes identify the files used; they do not certify source completeness.
